Email updates and your address.

What happens when you subscribe to YourMove Field Notes.

Field Notes is optional. Subscribing is not required to download, install, or use YourMove.

What we collect

When you subscribe, you submit your email address to Kit, the service YourMove uses to maintain its mailing list and deliver messages. Kit may also process information necessary to provide its service, including signup and confirmation status.

What we send

Field Notes covers job-application systems, practical guides, and occasional updates about YourMove, including releases and new resources. We do not use the signup form to collect résumés, job-application answers, or employer-site credentials.

Confirmation and unsubscribe

Kit sends a confirmation email before you become an active subscriber when double opt-in is enabled. Every marketing message will include an unsubscribe option. You may also contact support@yourmove.run about your email subscription. The service may retain limited suppression information to respect unsubscribe requests.

Where the information goes

Kit processes the signup and subscriber list on YourMove's behalf. For more about Kit's data handling, see Kit's privacy information and its subscriber-data processing terms.

This notice addresses the Field Notes email signup. It is not a complete policy for every feature of the marketing website or desktop application.
