The Manifesto

The YourMove Journal

The form has amnesia.

You don't. Your work history hasn't changed just because the next company uses a different form.

Upload your résumé.
Now re-type your résumé.

The joke would be better if it weren't a standard application experience. Maybe the hiring systems can't remember what you've told them. That's their problem. YourMove is built so it doesn't have to be yours.

The tool we wanted to exist.

One place to keep the jobs, résumés, examples, and answers that go into an application. Something that helps with repetitive forms where it can, tells you when it can't, and leaves the real decision with you.

It won't promise an interview. It won't invent your accomplishments. And it won't fire off applications without you.
