WHY I'M BUILDING YOURMOVE

I'm Joshua Jenks. I started YourMove while looking for work myself. After enough applications asked me to repeat experience I'd already documented, I wanted a better way to keep that work useful.

It's an independent, free and open-source project, and I'm still building it.
