How it works

Follow one
application.
Start to sent.

A role you want. A résumé you already wrote. A few answers worth using again.

Here’s how those pieces come together in YourMove—and where you take over on the employer’s website.

Walk through the application ↓
Save & reviewChoose your materialPrepare & inspectSubmit & record

First, keep
the opportunity.

Paste the job posting URL to save it. Retrieve details from a supported posting source, then check the title, employer, and description.

If retrieval can’t help, paste the description yourself. The job is still worth keeping.

Retrieving a posting and preparing its application form are separate capabilities. One working doesn’t prove the other will.

Choose what
goes with you.

Prepare your master and role-specific résumés in your usual editor. Import PDF or DOCX files into YourMove, then select and save the exact document for this application.

Candidate profile

Review and approve your saved contact details before authorizing their transfer.

Answer Library & Career Evidence

Use previous writing and reviewed experiences as references. Adapt the answer to the question in front of you.

See what’s worth retaining

Prepared locally.
Still yours to inspect.

YourMove can inspect selected employer forms and help fill supported controls after you authorize the transfer. Read the result before continuing: saved, transferred, and submitted are different states.

Authorize preparation. Then check what happened.

Review the selected material before transfer, then inspect the employer form itself.

Ready to review
Eligible saved details and answers can be transferred to supported fields. Check the values and selected résumé.
Needs your input
“Why this role?” still needs an answer. Missing information stays a task for you.
Not yet observed
Later steps, conditional questions, or embedded fields may remain unknown. Continue checking on the employer site.

The saved work is still useful.

When automated preparation isn’t supported, open the application and complete it yourself.

Available locally
Your selected résumé, saved profile, and prepared writing remain available to reference.
Complete manually
Attach the document and copy the answers you want to use. YourMove does not fill this site automatically.
Check on the site
Read every field and finish the employer’s remaining steps. A saved job is not a submitted application.

Actual support depends on the employer page, controls, and application release. Read the first-application guide.

You make
the final click.

On the employer’s website

Review the answers, attachments, and anything still unresolved. Submit the application yourself when you’re satisfied.

Then, back in YourMove

Record that you completed the application. Keep the job, document selection, and application history together.
