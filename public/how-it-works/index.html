THE FOUR STEPS

YOU DID THE WORK.
WHY START FROM ZERO?

A new employer form doesn't know your history, your best examples, or which résumé you want to use. YourMove gives you a place to keep those decisions and carry them forward.

01 Save the job 02 Bring your work 03 Prepare the form 04 Make the call
SAVE THE JOB

FIND A JOB
WORTH YOUR TIME.
KEEP THE DETAILS.

Save an opportunity you actually want. When a posting comes from a supported source, YourMove can retrieve useful job details so you don't have to rebuild the listing by hand.

Keep the posting in your queue, correct anything that's wrong, and come back when you're ready.

Some sources provide structured details. Others need manual entry. Either way, you decide what stays in your record.

PREPARE THE FORM

NEW COMPANY.
NEW FORM.
NOT A NEW START.

On supported employer pages, ask YourMove to help transfer eligible details and prepared answers into the form. It can reduce the typing. It can't replace the judgment.

Some fields still need you. Review what was filled, make corrections, and finish the unanswered parts on the actual employer site.

REVIEW AND RECORD

THE LAST CLICK
IS YOURS.
SO IS THE RECORD.

Read the finished application where it matters: on the employer's website. You choose whether to submit it.

Afterward, confirm that you applied in YourMove. The job, selected materials, and application history remain in your local workspace so the work can help the next time.

Recording an application in YourMove is your confirmation of what happened. It is not a receipt from the employer.
