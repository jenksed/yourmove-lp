DEFIANT.
NOT CHAOTIC.

A deliberately editorial identity for a product that carries work forward. Ink and paper create the stage; forest green signals agency, cobalt distinguishes applicant evidence, and orange is reserved for friction.

01 / THE ARGUMENT

You're not starting from zero.

Sharper than aspirational productivity language. Plain benefit tied to a familiar, repeated task.

02 / THE PHYSICAL METAPHOR

Proof and paperwork.

Document shapes, numbering, stamps, rules, and a restrained physical image direction. No interchangeable SaaS cards.

03 / THE PRODUCT PROOF

Show the user action.

Interactive, schematic workflow depicts actual app concepts without pretending to be a verified screenshot.

04 / THE BLUE RULE

Cobalt has a job.

Evidence records, provenance labels, applicant review. It does not mean independent verification or company-wide trust.
