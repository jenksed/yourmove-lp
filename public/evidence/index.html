YOUR
EXPERIENCE
IS THE ASSET.

A good application answer begins with something you actually did. Keep the substance behind the story, not just a polished paragraph.

CURATED BY THE APPLICANT / NOT THIRD-PARTY VERIFIED
IT'S MORE THAN A PILE OF ANSWERS

DON'T JUST
SAVE THE
PARAGRAPH.

Keep the situation, what you personally did, and any outcome you can support. YourMove's Career Evidence lets you curate those details independently of any one application.

When you face a similar question later, that record can give you an honest starting point. You decide what belongs in the answer.

EXAMPLE / EMPLOYER QUESTION

Tell us about a difficult technical problem you solved.

A familiar question. The answer you write now shouldn't be buried in yesterday's application.

ILLUSTRATIVE SCENARIO
EVIDENCE HAS STRUCTURETHE POINT IS ACCURACY, NOT POLISH FOR ITS OWN SAKE.
01

Situation

What was happening and why it needed attention.

02

Your actions

What you investigated, implemented, explained, or resolved.

03

Outcome

What actually happened, or an explicit “unknown” when the result isn't known.

04

Review

Your confirmation that the record accurately describes your experience.

What cobalt means on this site. An applicant-authored evidence record or a statement about its source and review state. “Reviewed” means the applicant confirmed the content, not that an employer or third party verified it.
