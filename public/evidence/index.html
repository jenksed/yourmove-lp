Approved facts & reviewed experiences

You approve candidate information and mark experiences as reviewed. Draft experiences can stay incomplete; editing a reviewed experience returns it to draft.

YourMove preserves your account of the work. It does not verify employment, open supporting references, or independently establish that a claim is true.

AI suggestions

Optional AI assistance can propose wording. A proposal is not approved evidence and should not be treated as a fact because it sounds plausible.

Review sources, correct the writing, and decide what to adopt and save. You remain responsible for every claim. External providers may receive selected information after you authorize generation.
