WHY IT EXISTS / 02THE PROBLEM WASN'T THE RESUME.

SAME PERSON.
ANOTHER
BLANK FORM.

Looking for work is already a lot of work. You research the company, decide whether a role fits, and try to explain what you've actually done. Then a new hiring platform asks you to enter your history and answers all over again.

I'm a systems and support engineer. Repeated manual work is the kind of problem I naturally want to fix. This one was personal.

That's where YourMove came from: keep the useful information, bring it into the next application, and let the applicant review what goes out.
