WHY THIS EXISTS

SAME PERSON.
ANOTHER
BLANK FORM.

Looking for work is already a lot of work. You research the company, decide whether a role fits, and try to explain what you've actually done. Then a new hiring platform asks you to enter your history and answers all over again.

After another application asked for information I had already written, I stopped thinking about the next blank field and started thinking about a better way to carry my work forward.

That became YourMove: keep useful information, bring it into supported forms, and leave the final answer and submission to the applicant.
